Mataji's Sthan

The devasthan of Shri Siriyal Mataji at Bhinsara, Kutch: where it is, how to reach it, what it looks like today and what it looked like before the temple was rebuilt.

Where it is

Bhinsara (Bhisara) is a village in Kutch district, Gujarat. The devasthan is a small place, not easy to find without directions, and many of our own brethren have reached the village and still struggled to locate it. The map and directions below are meant to fix that.

The temple today and before

The present structure is a rebuilt temple. The original sthan was simpler and looked different, and the Mandal has kept old photographs and records of it. Both belong on this page: the Mata as she is housed today, and the sthan as our grandparents knew it.
