Disclaimer

What these pages are, what they are not, and how to challenge anything on them.

Read this first

This is research, done pro bono, by members of the Savla community for the community. It has been compiled from the documents listed on the Research & sources page: a Barot notebook copied from the Jamnagar Bhatt Raja's chopdi, the Anchalgachha Mahoti Pattavali, the Chheda vanshvruksh, and published histories of Kutch. Where the pages say something is written in the chopdi or the pattavali, that is what they mean: it is written there. Being written is not the same as being proven.

There is no guarantee of completeness or accuracy. Barot records are oral genealogy set down late; pattavalis were compiled centuries after the events they describe; no contemporary inscription has yet been found for the 13th-century conversions. The pages state their main account, list the alternates, and mark what remains open. They will change as research continues.

Anyone may challenge any line. If your family holds a chopda, a document or a tradition that says something different, the Mandal wants to see it. Send it through the Community page. A credible challenge will be recorded on the alternate accounts page and, if it proves stronger, will replace the main account.

Nothing here is a legal or religious ruling. These pages do not decide questions of gotra, marriage, inheritance or ritual eligibility, and they are not a substitute for the Bhatt Raja's Chopda, which remains the authoritative record of lineage.

Sources are credited; originals stay with their owners. Transcriptions are the Mandal's own and may contain reading errors, marked (?) where known. Scans of the originals will be attached so readers can check for themselves.
