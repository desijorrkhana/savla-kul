Community

Savla households are spread across Kutch, Mumbai and the world. This page is for reaching them, and for them to reach back: with records, corrections, photographs and news.

Write to the Mandal

If you hold an old chopda, a family tree, a photograph of the Mata or the old temple, or a different account of our origin than the one on these pages, the Mandal wants to hear from you. Fill in the form; it prepares a message you can send by WhatsApp or email to the contacts on the Contact page.

Your message

Copy this and send it to any Mandal contact by WhatsApp or email. Attach scans or photos in the same message.

Where Savlas are

From the 52 Kutch villages and 24 Vagad villages of the Barot record, Savla families today live in Mumbai, across Gujarat, and in East Africa, the UK, the Gulf and North America. The Mandal is compiling a directory of households by place so that the Pehdi and Juvar reach everyone. If your household is not yet enrolled, use the form above.
