Research & sources

Every document behind these pages, what each one actually says, and where to look further. Readings marked (?) are uncertain; dates are Vikram Samvat unless marked CE.

Primary

The three documents

A
Savla Utpatti notebook
19 handwritten pages copied from the chopdi of the Jamnagar Bhatt Raja (Bharu) family. Origin, the Sonapur legend, migration, the 84-nukh tables, a name table. 20th-century oral-genealogical record.
B
Anchalgachha Mahoti Pattavali
The gachha's succession history. Jayasimhasuri's conversions (p. 175), the Dedhia section (pp. 176–177: Jaisalmer, Samvat 1255), the Sonapur episode under Ajitsimhasuri (pp. 215–217). Compiled 17th–19th c., printed 20th c.
C
Chheda vanshvruksh
Poster by Bhatt Raja Bipinkumar Vishanjibhai, Khambhalia Naka, Jamnagar, for the Chheda family of Bhadia. Devaḍaji's conversion at Patan, VS 1255, Bhadarva Sud 5; his four sons and their nukhs.

A · The Savla Utpatti notebook

Origin of the kul pp. 1–3

  • Kul Chavda; gotra Kashyap; Suryavansh; Yajurved; guru Vashishtha. Original seat Ayodhya, then Patan on the Sarasvati; kuldev Kamleshwar Mahadev.
  • Line begins from Vanraj Chavda, "born about 1254 years ago from today"; Shilgunsuri named as his guru.
  • Conversion: VS 1255, from the updesh of Jayasimhasuri, at Patan — "Umeraj no dikro Devalji" (Deval = Devaḍ).
  • From Chavda, four shakhas: Chheda (Amba), Nandu (Momai), Savla (Sarayal), Dedhia (Mamat/Mamal).
  • The Samat Sahu / Sonapur legend, in full on the alternate accounts page.

Migration pp. 4–9

સંવત ૧૨૫૫ માં જૈન ધર્મનો અંગીકાર કર્યો. કેટલાક વરસો બાદ મારવાડમાં ઓસિયા નગરીમાં રહ્યા, ત્યાર બાદ થરપારકરમાં રહ્યા. ત્યાં મુસલમાનોનો રાજ હતો… માજનોને કાધેલ (કહેલ) જો અહીં રહેવું હોય તો રોટી-બેટીનો વહેવાર કરવો પડશે અને તમોને કલમાં પડવું પડશે… માજનોએ કહ્યું કે અહિંસા પરમો ધર્મનો ત્યાગ કરી કલમાં કેમ પડાય? બાદશાહે અમારા ઉપર ચોકી-પહેરો ગોઠવી રાખેલ છે, કોઈ અમને સહારો નથી. ત્યારે ભાટ (અને ગોરજી) બે ભાઈઓ સતમરામ કુમાર સુમરો, જે હિંદુ ધર્મી હતા, ત્યાં ગયા; બિરદાવલી ગાઈને ખુશી કર્યા; કુમાર સુમરાએ માગવા કહ્યું ત્યારે ભાટે વચન માગ્યું કે ઓસવાળોને મુક્ત અપાવો… ભાટ માજનોને લેવા ગયેલ, ત્યાં પહેરેગીરોને જોઈ ગુરુજીને સૂચન કર્યું કે "હું મારી ફરજ પૂરી કરીને આવ્યો છું, હવે તમારું કામ તમે કરો." ગુરુજી સાહેબે પોતાના બાધા(વ્રત)ના પ્રતાપથી પહેરેગીરોને આંધળા તથા બહેરા બનાવી દીધા. માજનોએ ઉચાળા ભર્યા… ઝૂંપડાવાળા હતા, બળદગાડાં ઉપર પોતાનો ઘરબાર વસાવેલ.

Transcribed from the two notebook pages shown to the Mandal; cursive and faded in places, uncertain words normalised. The earlier summary on these pages had read the rescuers' names as "Aagar Kumaravar and Satmaram"; the pages themselves name the Sumra prince as Satmaram Kumar Sumro and the two brothers as the Bhat and the Gorji.

  • Osian → Thar Parkar under Muslim rule; the choice of roti-beti and the kalma or leaving; the Bhat and Gorji win the Oswals' release from Satmaram Kumar Sumro; the Guruji disables the guards; the mahajans leave on bullock carts. The rescuers' names are written first at any auspicious work.
  • → Kanthkot (Garkapuri, seat of the Kanthnath mahant), c. VS 1300–1454; divided by Jam Dedaji's family, the Kutch naresh and the Rayal Jam of Bara among Kandi, Sakhda-sama and the Garh. First Vagad village Lakadia.
  • Halar with Jam Rayal (Jam Rawal, 1540): Kansumra near Jamnagar.
  • Four divisions: Kutch 52, Vagad 24, Sakhda 42, Halar; 170 villages.

The uchala duha community oral tradition

A nine-verse Kutchi duha with the refrain "ke Osvale uchala", dating the leaving of Sindh to Samvat 1350 and naming its causes: Khilji's governors, the Sayyids, broken temples and treasuries, raids, the slaughter of cattle, no justice, "convert or die", children taken and sold by Arabs. Full text and translation on the Utpatti page. Provenance (which family or Barot preserved it) to be recorded.

Last page p. 19

Name and telephone of a Jamnagar Bharu, and a Barot kavit — identifying the source as the same Jamnagar Bhatt Raja gharana that prepared the Chheda vanshvruksh.

B · Anchalgachha Mahoti Pattavali

Jayasimhasuri, 2nd pattadhar pp. 64–86, 175

  • Born VS 1179 at Sopara; diksha VS 1197 at Tharad under Aryarakshitasuri; acharya-pad VS 1202 at Mandor; at Patan in VS 1224 before Kumarapala. Died VS 1258 at Prabhas Patan, aged 80.
  • Conversions listed (p. 175): Hathundia 1208, Gala 1211, Pidhaiya 1224, Nagda 1228, Lalan 1229, Sahasgana Gandhi 1231, Dedhia 1255 (an earlier transcription on these pages read this as 1245; corrected against the Dedhia section and the Digdarshan).

Dedhia gotra pp. 176–177

Devaḍ Chavda, at Jaisalmer, takes the twelve vratas from Jayasimhasuri in VS 1255 and enters the Oswal jnati. Kutch villages of the Dedhia stock: Dabhasang, Sonardi, Bhujpur, Baladia, Deshalpur, Bhorara, Bhadana and others.

Ajitsimhasuri, 6th pattadhar pp. 215–217

The Sonapur episode: Purnachandra shravak's Shatrunjay sangh; Samarsimha, Chavda kshatriya, attacks at the lake; stambhan by invoking Mahakali; mother Sulochana's plea; conversion on the vow of jeevahimsa-tyag. No gotra named. Also VS 1336, Adinath pratishtha at Naraili by Bhusa shravak.

C · The Chheda vanshvruksh

  • "At Anhilpur Patan in Samvat 1255, Bhadarva sud 5, hearing the updesh of the twelve vratas from Shri Jaysinhsuriji, Devaḍaji Chavda accepted the Jain dharma."
  • Four sons: Jamarsinh → Dedhia; Jetsinh → Savla; Padamsinh → Nandu; Samarsinh → Chheda.
  • Gotra box: Kashyap, Suryavansh, Yajurved, Kamleshwar Mahadev; Ayodhya → Patan.
  • Chheda line to fifteen generations. The equivalent Savla list from Jetsinh is the document the Mandal most needs; contacts on the poster: 96198 88206 (Mumbai), 99255 60542 / 94261 22556 (Jamnagar).

Where to look next

  • Anchalgachha Digdarshan (અંચલગચ્છ દિગ્દર્શન, sachitra edition, by "Parshva", 1968; scan from Shree Sudharmaswami Gyanbhandar, Umara, Surat) — the fullest modern survey. ¶346 (p. 80): Jaisalmer, Samvat 1255, Devaḍ Chavda Rajput converted by Jayasimhasuri and brought into the Oswal jnati. ¶347: his son Āmar builds the Adinath temple at Jalore. ¶348: Āmar's son Dedhia names the gotra; descendant Jethanand Sheth leads a Shatrunjay sangh. ¶349–350: the Dedhiya-village variant, Samvat 1254. ¶351: Kuldevi Mamal Mata. ¶352: the gotra among Kutchi Oswals and Marwad Parmars. ¶381–382 (pp. 85–86): Rao Keshav of Kotda, Bhadarva Sud 5, 1259; and the Kartak-adi / Chaitra-adi / Ashadh-adi samvat drift. The Ajitsimhasuri chapter (pp. 134–149) may locate Sonapur.
  • Chavda Vansh (PDF book) — a web resource on the Chavda dynasty cited by the Mandal; to be catalogued with author and link.
  • Anchalgachha publications — Amarsagarsuri's Vardhamana-Padmasimha Shreshthi Charitra; Bhavsagarsuri's Gurvavali.
  • Inscriptions — Jalore Adinath temple inscriptions as a candidate for Devaḍ-era references. None found yet.
  • History of Kutch — Chavda chiefs at Patgadh and Vagham to the 13th c.; Jam Dedaji of Kanthkot; Jam Rawal of Nawanagar (1540).
