Vidhi

The Mandal's observances as printed in its book: the daily puja and aarti of Shri Siriyal Mataji, the Navratri vidhi, the Pehdi and the Juvar. The tradition of prayer, as it has come down to us.

The annual observances

પેઢી
Pehdi · Mumbai
Held every year at Mumbai on the traditional mahurat. The gathering of the Savla households resident in Mumbai before the Mata.
જુવાર
Juvar · Bhinsara
Held every year at the devasthan at Bhinsara on the traditional mahurat. The kul's visit to the Mata at her own sthan.
નવરાત્રિ
Navratri
Nine nights of the Mata's worship, with the garba and aarti as the community has always done them.
To be transcribed from the Mandal's printed book. Family rites recorded in the Chopda

Janam, lagna and maran of Savla households are recorded by the Bhatt Raja in the Chopda, so that the line from 1565 continues unbroken. To have an event recorded, or to obtain a certified extract of your lineage, contact the Mandal. The customary offering to the Bhatt Raja and the Mandal's procedure will be stated here.
